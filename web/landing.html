01 — The problem

A spot takes minutes to render. The first reviewable take lands in under ten seconds — and you can already watch it, judge it and send it back.

Every scene is encoded and pushed to Backblaze B2 the moment it exists. The player attaches to a growing HLS playlist, so review starts while the render is still running. Reject a take and the refined one joins the same playlist — no reload, no second link.
